# Writing

## Voice

Write like a sharp friend who did the homework: plain words, specific facts, short sentences. The parents are smart and busy.

- Never use em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
- Lead with the answer. "Juniper Lane is the best fit" before the reasons.
- Numbers beat adjectives: "a ten-year average teacher tenure", not "experienced staff".
- No stock phrases: delve, tapestry, foster, nurture a love of learning, journey, thrive, vibrant, holistic, unlock, empower, navigate, landscape, "not just X but Y", "it's important to note".
- No triplets of adjectives. No rhetorical questions.
- Name uncertainty once, where it applies: "tuition from a third-party listing; confirm with the school".

## Summary (Overview tab)

- `headline`: the decision in under ten words.
- `body`: two or three short paragraphs. Who fits best and why, what the main trade-off is, what to do next.
- `recommendation`: ordered, one sentence each, saying why that school and what to do about it.

## Finances summary

One headline and one short paragraph. Name the schools that are well funded, the one to watch, and the ones with no visibility. Explain any jargon (net assets: what the school owns minus what it owes).

## Essays

Each finalist with an essay prompt gets a draft in `essays`.

- Written as the parents ("we", "our"), in the child's corner.
- Open with something specific the child does, not a summary of their personality.
- Tie the child to one or two concrete things about this school's program, from your research.
- Use only facts the parents gave you. Wherever a real memory or detail would help and you don't have one, leave a bracketed slot in capitals: `[A MOMENT FROM THE TOUR]`, `[SOMETHING SHE LOVES TO BUILD]`. Never invent an anecdote.
- Stay under the word limit. Default 250.
- Different schools get genuinely different drafts, not one essay with the name swapped.

The report page has a "Redraft with Claude" button that rewrites a draft from the parents' own paragraph about their child under these same rules.
